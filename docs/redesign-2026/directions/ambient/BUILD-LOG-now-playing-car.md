# Ambient Now Playing, car posture: build decisions

Written by the build agent (branch `redesign/ambient-now-playing-car`). Calls made without the owner or the art director in the loop; each can be reversed here.

## What was built

`ui/car.js` owns the posture (`<html data-posture="car">` is the whole state), the 600ms hold on the mini row, the medium haptic hook, `?posture=car`, and the car chip. `ui/car.css` owns the glance layout. `player/client.js` gained five small hooks (adopt twice, `bindPress` on the bar, end posture when the sheet collapses and when playback stops, open the sheet once when `?posture=car` is already on the page). `now-playing` step `now-playing-car` is appended to the `player` state in `tools/ui-lab/lib/states.mjs`; `screens.json` has `now-playing-car` (393x852) and `now-playing-car-375` (shoot it with `--viewports 375x667`).

## Calls that differ from a literal reading of the notes

1. **This branch contains a merge of `origin/redesign/ambient-now-playing` (45a89698).** Car posture is a mode of the Now Playing sheet, which was not yet on the direction branch when this unit started. Only registration lists (index.html, SHELL lists, the budget table, `states.mjs`, two token-test lines) conflicted with Today, and were unioned. The Dock branch (`origin/redesign/ambient-dock`) was NOT merged: it conflicts with Today in `ui/tabbar.js`, `player/client.js` and `styles.css`, which is that unit's to resolve. Its long-press is a second implementation of the same gesture; when both land, keep one `bindPress` call (this one lives in `ui/car.js` and is tested on a fake DOM and in Chromium) and drop the other.
2. **Posture ends when the sheet collapses**, as the Dock branch also decides. A posture that outlived the sheet would hide the tab bar with no chip on screen to bring it back.
3. **Entering never waits on the Dock.** The Dock-out rules (`.dock`, `.dock-fade`, `.dock-cast`) already sit in `ui/tokens.css`; today's `#tab-bar`, which the Dock replaces, is hidden by `ui/car.css` as well. The mini row is left in place (the sheet makes it inert and covers it) so the shared-element move from its art still has a source.
4. **The artwork stays 240 (200 under 700px tall) even when a three-line title would shrink it to 180** in the normal posture (`is-long-title`). Under a user text scale (`:root[data-type-scale]`) the 160 floor still wins, by source order: accessibility over the posture.
5. **The 200 starts under 700px, not at 700.** `max-height: 699.98px`, because the acceptance says "under 700px tall". The title clamp is `min-height: 800px` for three lines, two below.
6. **The dots are not rendered in the car** (they open the detail posture, which is not rendered). The chip takes their place at the top right.
7. **The chip** sits in the middle of the head as the prototype's does (the head becomes a `1fr auto 1fr` grid in the car; the grabber is absolutely placed and takes no cell) and is a bg2 pill, not Lamp (the first build made it Lamp-filled at the right edge: a 134px region delta). It is a 44px button drawing a 36px pill (`::before`), so the hit box meets the floor and the pill stays light. `aria-pressed="true"` because posture is on; named "Car mode on. Leave car mode". Its label is `--t-label`, so it grows x1.25 with the rest, where the prototype's `.chip` fixes 14px: that is the 8px width delta in the fidelity table. It adds no transition, so the one reduced-motion block has nothing of this file to name.
8. **"No ellipsis beside empty Room space"** is measured as: at 800px and taller the clamp is 3, an ellipsis only ever appears on the clamp's last line, and a short title shows one line with none. At 393x852 a title that needs more than three lines still ends in an ellipsis with about 85px of free Room under it; that is the plan's number (three lines), not a defect the build can close without changing it.
9. **Haptics:** the hook calls `Capacitor.Plugins.Haptics.impact({ style: "MEDIUM" })` only when the native shell has put the plugin on the page; there is no `ui/haptics.js` yet (BUILD-PLAN item 3, Phase 5), so on the web and in the lab it is a no-op.
10. **Browser facts live in `tools/ui-lab/car-check.mjs`**, not in a Playwright spec, because CI's `data-and-site` job has no Chromium. The pure rules it applies (`lib/car-rules.mjs`) are unit-tested in CI (`car-check.test.mjs`, floor 13). Run it before pushing a change to this screen: `node tools/ui-lab/car-check.mjs --no-remote-images`.

## Iteration 3 (fidelity findings: lit-art rim, grabber contrast)

- **The dark rim was two things, both gone.** The car's restated lit-art cast still led with `var(--shadow-1)` (a black 1px/2px drop), and styles.css gives `.fp-s-art` (the sleeve) a 1px `--line` border. Both removed in `ui/car.css`: the cast is the art's colour only (reduced transparency drops it to nothing, not to the black rim) and the sleeve has `border: 0`. Measured on the render: the 2 device-pixel dark edge under the art is gone. The same two rules still sit in `now-playing.css` for the normal posture; that screen's file is not this unit's, so it is a follow-up for whoever next touches Now Playing (a one-line change there, affecting only the normal posture).
- **Grabber** is `--text-2` at full opacity (was `--text-3` at 70%, a few percent off the Glow-tinted Room) and sits at the prototype's `top: -6px` of the head. Contrast of `--text-2` on the Room's lightest stop is above 3:1 (non-text).
- Fidelity run `now-playing-car-i3` / `now-playing-car-375-i3` (dark): region deltas unchanged from iteration 2 (play, skips, close 0; art/title/chip as explained above); car-check clean (12 evaluations); gates new 0.

## Iteration 4 (review: bundle cap, Room, long-title rhythm)

Decisions by the art director, owner asleep:

1. **Bundle over the 3 MB cap (3,718 B).** Fixed at the source: the shell-only `kokoro-probe-passage.json` was copied pretty-printed (35 KB); `prepare()` now serialises it compact like every `data/*.json` beside it (14 KB, the page reads it with `fetch().json()`), a 21 KB saving that also clears the 2.85 MB headroom alarm. Test `the shell-only probe passage ships compact...` names its two mutations. No cap or budget was raised.
2. **Room lit from above.** `ui/now-playing.css` gains the prototype's `.room-bg::before` (a Glow radial, 55% at 50% / 18%) as the first child of the Room layers, so the top zone is a warm light and not flat grey under dull artwork. Hue is still data.
3. **Long-title rhythm: reverses decision 4 above.** A three-line title now steps the artwork to 180 at every height (DIRECTION), instead of keeping 240 and squeezing the gaps. `car.css`, `car-rules.mjs` and both tests changed together; `car-check` is clean at 393x852 and 375x667.
4. **Chip 80x44 against the prototype's 72x36, and the title's extra height** stay: the 44px tap floor and the x1.25 label, and the fixture's three-line title against the prototype's two. The title's height is the data, not the layout.
5. **Not this unit's:** the three Discover gate entries, the transient `ERR_NO_BUFFER_SPACE`, the Now Playing baseline diffs (that unit rides in this branch's diff until it merges), the voice-settings guard and the three deploy-id tests (Windows CRLF checkout).

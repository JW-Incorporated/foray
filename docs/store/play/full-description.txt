4a picks podcast episodes for you and puts them into four topic queues. Not a feed. Not a wall of recommendations. Four cards on one screen — and then you are done choosing.

Three of the four are subjects you already listen to. The fourth is marked Stretch: something next door to your usual taste rather than more of the same, so there is still room to be surprised.

WHAT EACH CARD TELLS YOU BEFORE YOU TAP
Every card shows how many episodes are in the queue, how long the whole thing runs, which shows it draws from, and the title of the first episode. Nothing is a mystery box. You can see what you would be getting and skip it if today is not the day.

Tap one and you get the queue itself: episodes in order, with the show and the running time for each.

ASK FOR SOMETHING SPECIFIC
Not in the mood for any of the four? Type what you actually want — the Roman empire, how engines work, something about the deep sea — and 4a assembles a queue for it from its library. Come back for a fresh set of cards, or tap refresh for a different four.

A PLAYER THAT STAYS OUT OF THE WAY
Listen inside 4a with a scrubber, back 15 seconds, forward 30 seconds and adjustable playback speed. It remembers where you stopped. Or hand the episode over to Apple Podcasts — or open the show in Pocket Casts — if that is where you keep your listening.

SAVE IT
Star an episode to keep it, and 4a leans a little further into that subject next time. Nothing you have to fill in, no profile to build.

FAMILY MODE
One switch hides episodes rated explicit, and the whole comedy category with them.

NO ACCOUNT. NO ADS.
No sign-up, no password, no email address, nothing to verify. 4a works the moment you open it. There is no advertising in the app, and 4a runs no ad tracking. Your library stays readable with no signal — on a plane, in a tunnel — though playing audio still needs a connection. And "Delete my data" sits in the menu, not four screens deep in a settings tree.

FOUR, NOT FOREVER
No streaks. No infinite scroll. No autoplay chain into the next thing. No notifications badgering you back. 4a is finished when you have listened to what you chose, and that is the whole idea.

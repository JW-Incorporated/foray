4a is a podcast app built on one idea: a good subject is bigger than any single show.

FORAYS: ONE SUBJECT, MANY SHOWS
A Foray takes one subject and plays moments from several podcasts about it, one after another, each straight from that show's own feed. Find them under Forays for you on Home, or on the Forays page in the menu. 4a puts each Foray together by hand, so they arrive a few at a time.

HOME
Open 4a and there is a Play button ready to go, then Jump back in for whatever you left partway, Forays for you, Playlists for you, and Suggested episodes. Forays for you and Suggested each keep a place for a pick marked Stretch: something outside your usual subjects, with a line saying why it is there.

BUILD A PLAYLIST
Not seeing what you want? On Create, name a subject (the semiconductor supply chain, the Roman empire) and 4a builds a playlist for it from across its catalogue. Save the ones worth keeping. Search finds shows and episodes.

LISTEN RIGHT HERE
Episodes play inside 4a, with no hand-off to another app: back 15 seconds, forward 30, playback speed, and chapters on episodes whose publishers include them. 4a remembers where you stopped in each one.

UP NEXT, AND THEN MORE
Add episodes to Up Next, or tap Play next to put one at the front. When an episode ends, 4a keeps going: the rest of Up Next, then the rest of the list you started from, then more episodes that fit what you listen to. Would rather it stopped? Turn Continuous playback off in the menu.

DOWNLOADS FOR OFFLINE LISTENING
Tap Download on an episode's page to keep it on your phone and play it with no signal. You choose every download; 4a never downloads anything on its own. Downloads wait for Wi-Fi unless you turn on Download over cellular, and you can remove one, or all of them, from your Library.

SAVE AND FOLLOW
Tap the star to save an episode to your Library, and 4a leans a little further toward that subject next time. Follow a show to keep it one tap away in your Library, marked when it has new episodes. Following queues nothing and sends no phone notification.

SHARE
Send a Foray, a playlist, an episode or a show to someone as a link.

FAMILY MODE
One switch in the menu hides episodes rated explicit, the whole comedy category, and any episode without a rating unless its show is rated clean.

NO ACCOUNT. NO ADS.
No sign-up, no password, no email address, nothing to verify. 4a works the moment you open it. There is no advertising in 4a, and no ad tracking or analytics SDK inside it. Episodes play from each publisher's own servers exactly as published, so any ads a show runs itself are still there. Most of what 4a knows about you stays on your phone, and Delete my data in the menu clears your phone, deletes your rows on 4a's server, and tells you if anything could not be cleared.

NO STREAKS. NO ENDLESS FEED.
4a keeps no streaks, has no infinite scroll, and sends no notifications to pull you back in.

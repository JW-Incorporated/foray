# Persona-rating gold set (RV-19), 2026-10-15

Card RV-19 of [`docs/roadmap/recommender-v1.md`](../roadmap/recommender-v1.md), issue #1380. It follows the pilot ([`persona-ratings-pilot-2026-10.md`](persona-ratings-pilot-2026-10.md), #1401) and makes the three changes the pilot asked for: the joint design, a 100-show isolated slice for the four flagged personas, and a lock in `progress.mjs` (#1403).

The orchestrator ran it on the subscription as plan decision D1 specifies. Claude Code agents (Sonnet 5.5) followed [`docs/agents/runner-prompts/persona-rate-batch.md`](../agents/runner-prompts/persona-rate-batch.md) v1, with no API call and no network access during rating.

The block between the `agreement:gold` markers is generated by `tools/recommender/agreement.mjs`. Everything outside it is written by hand. The offline eval was re-run on these ratings ([`persona-ratings-eval-2026-10.md`](persona-ratings-eval-2026-10.md)).

## Verdict: the gold set is usable as the student's target

1. **Consistency holds at scale.**
   - Test-retest alpha on `fit` is **0.888** over 499 shows rated in two or three joint passes, against 0.908 on the pilot's 59. Per persona it ranges from 0.79 (family-listener) to 0.92 (banter-regular).
   - No batch drifts: the largest deviation of a batch mean from the overall mean is 0.198, against the D13 bar of 0.3.
2. **Joint rating is safe for 12 of 13 personas.**
   - Rule: a persona is flagged when joint-vs-isolated Spearman falls more than 0.10 below its own joint-vs-joint retest. Here the retest baseline is the mean of passes 1-2 and 1-3.
   - Banter-regular, practical-improver and true-crime-careful were flagged in the pilot. At n = 98 they now sit within noise: gaps 0.043, 0.018 and 0.069.
   - **Slow-news-contextualist stays flagged, marginally.** Its gap is 0.106, but only against pass 2 (0.136); against pass 3 it is 0.076. Its disagreement is also **unbiased**:
     - mean fit is 1.52 both joint and isolated;
     - of the shows where the isolated fit differs, 12 are higher and 12 lower;
     - 76% of fits match exactly.
   - That reads as noise in a narrow persona, not joint-context pressure. By the pilot's rule this earns a follow-up card: a persona-group mode in `prepare-rating-batch.mjs`, re-rating this one persona in its own group call. **Recommendation: file it at low priority.** Nothing served depends on slow-news-contextualist's ties at fit 1-2.
   - True-crime-careful leans mildly the other way: isolated fits are higher on 12 shows and lower on 2 (mean 1.24 against 1.35). It is still under the bar. RV-25's student should be checked on true-crime-careful's 3+ subset.
3. **Personas are distinct, and none is redundant (input for RV-29).**
   - The variance ratio is **9.71**: personas disagree about a show far more than shows differ on average.
   - No pair correlates at r >= 0.85. The closest pairs are banter-regular/sharp-comedy (0.62) and narrative-binger/true-crime-careful (0.52), both intended adjacencies.
   - Four personas are rarely a show's top persona: true-crime-careful 0.6%, sharp-comedy 0.9%, family-listener 2.8% and history-deep-diver 2.9%.
   - This is the catalogue's base rate, not redundancy. Each one still fills a top-50 at fit 3-4, and each leads its intended roots: kids-family is 50% of family-listener's top 50, true-crime 62% of true-crime-careful's, and history 42% of history-deep-diver's.
   - **Recommendation for RV-29:** keep all 13 personas. Spend v1.1 on the archetype map instead (the eval's finding: commute-explainer belongs under `deep-learn`). Persona pruning would remove real listeners whom a random catalogue sample under-represents.
4. **Cost came in under the estimate.** The run took about **36M tokens** against the pilot's estimate of 40M, and about 8 hours of pool time (table below).

## Reading the rest of the numbers

- **Distribution.** Fit is heavily skewed, a little more than in the pilot:
  - The overall mean is 1.67 (pilot 1.74).
  - True-crime-careful is 86% ones, sharp-comedy 72% and history-deep-diver 71%.
  - Comfort-companion is the only persona centred on 2-3: 34% at its modal value.
  - This is the catalogue base rate. A stratified random sample of 26k shows holds few shows any narrow persona wants.
  - The student (RV-25) should report its gate on each persona's 3+ subset as well as overall, or a model predicting 1 everywhere will look good.
- **Top persona churn.** The top persona changes between passes for 36% of the retest shows (pilot: 32%; there are now three passes to differ). Almost all are near-ties at low fit. The pilot's rule stands: show `top_reason` only when the top fit is 4 or more.
- **Rubric hygiene.**
  - Confidence caps and audio nulls never triggered (`confidence_capped` 0, `audio_nulled` 0 across 239 merges). Every pack was metadata-tier: no transcript windows exist locally, and R2 is gated on #1163 17.3.
  - Content flags in pass 1: explicit 157, medical/financial advice 77, harassment 2.
  - Formats: interview 833, panel 737, mixed 577, monologue 433, narrative 289, news 108.
- **Injection: 3 flags, all correct and harmless.** None of the three shows' ratings look moved:
  - Eat Your Spanish (1479312117): its feed text addresses AI/ML models.
  - The State of The Universe (1429808898): episode descriptions ask listeners to rate it 5 stars. This is ordinary boilerplate, flagged because it names a score.
  - The Blue Room (1172351002): an episode description contains "CLAUDE AI".
  - Several raters also noted "rate and review" boilerplate elsewhere and correctly left it unflagged.
- **Catalogue label bugs raters found (for RV-26).**
  - Outrage + Optimism (1459416461): climate title, daily tech-news blurb. It was rated at confidence 0.15-0.2 in two passes, and one rater declined it.
  - Librivox Audiobooks (1680031836): the blurb describes credit and bond news.
  - Where Should We Begin, and Credentialing Chronicles (1801579797): the blurbs contradict the feeds.
  - 1685349616: the blurb says Persian, but the episodes are in English.
  - Several non-English shows (Mandarin, Russian, Vietnamese, Persian, Portuguese, Italian) were rated at accessibility 1 and low confidence instead of being skipped. That matches the rubric, but RV-26 may want a language field.

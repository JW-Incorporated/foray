# Persona-rating student (RV-25), 2026-10

Card RV-25 of [`docs/roadmap/recommender-v1.md`](../roadmap/recommender-v1.md), issue #1380. It trains RV-14's student (`tools/recommender/student.mjs`, ridge from features to the persona rating vector) on the RV-19 gold set minus its holdout ([`persona-ratings-gold-2026-10.md`](persona-ratings-gold-2026-10.md)). It then scores the student on the 495 rated holdout shows with RV-14's gate.

The block between the `student:gate` markers is generated by `node tools/recommender/predict.mjs --train`. Everything outside it is written by hand. No model was called and nothing was fetched: the features are the committed catalogue files, the local RSS cache and the local RV-12 embeddings (`Xenova/bge-small-en-v1.5`, 384-dim, from the RV-19 run).

## Verdict: the gate fails, so nothing is predicted

1. **The student does not reach the gate.** All three deciding metrics miss, and RV-14's thresholds are used unchanged:
   - alpha on `fit` is 0.785 against 0.80, a near miss;
   - per-persona Spearman reaches 0.75 for only 3 of 13 personas (banter-regular 0.82, mechanism-hunter 0.76, practical-improver 0.76); the lowest is true-crime-careful at 0.52;
   - mean within-node tau is 0.51 against 0.60.
2. **Two sub-scores are dropped** and two are kept. `accessibility` (alpha 0.66) and `binge_vs_dip` (0.52) fall under 0.67. `topic_fit` (0.81) and `depth` (0.68) pass.
3. **`student-model.json` is committed with `gate.pass: false`.** `ratings-predicted.json` is not written, and `--predict` refuses. `--check` passes: the model is bound to the committed gold set by its sha256.
4. **The embeddings for the ~23k other catalogued shows were not computed.** Nothing would consume them while the gate fails.

## Why it fails, read against the teacher's own ceiling

- **The per-persona Spearman bar is above the teacher's own retest for two personas.**
  - The teacher agrees with itself across joint passes at 0.70-0.90 (the gold report's mean retest column, repeated in the last column below).
  - Family-listener (0.703) and true-crime-careful (0.719) sit under the 0.75 bar on retest alone. A student that copied a second teacher pass would still fail them.
  - The gate as written cannot pass for those two personas with any student. Changing the bar, for example to a fraction of each persona's retest, is a plan decision for the orchestrator. It is not made here.
- **Measured against the ceiling, the student recovers 72-91% of the teacher's retest Spearman.**
  - Best: banter-regular and mechanism-hunter (0.91), practical-improver (0.90).
  - Worst: history-deep-diver and true-crime-careful (0.72).
- **Most of the agreement comes from telling ones from the rest.** Fit is heavily skewed (over half the shows are ones for 10 of 13 personas, up to 86% for true-crime-careful), so the 3+ columns are the honest view of ranking among the shows a persona would actually be served:
  - On the shows the teacher rated 3 or more, Spearman falls to 0.06-0.67.
  - Sharp-comedy is 0.06 (n = 44): the student cannot order comedy shows for that persona.
  - The student under-calls good fits. Its mean on the 3+ subset is 2.2-3.0, and the 3+ MAE is 0.5-1.1. Ridge on a skewed target shrinks toward the mass at 1.
- **Lambda is not the issue.** Five-fold CV on the training rows picks 1,000, inside the grid, and the CV error is flat from 300 to 3,000.
- **Features match what the raters saw.** Rebuilt from the same RSS cache, every rated show's pack tier equals the tier recorded in `ratings.json` (2,800 `episode-metadata`, 178 `show-metadata`).

## What would have to change (not done here)

These are follow-ups for the orchestrator. None of them is in this card.

1. **Gate.** Decide whether the per-persona Spearman bar should be relative to each persona's retest ceiling. As written it is unreachable for family-listener and true-crime-careful.
2. **Student.** Candidates within D4 (d)'s "embedding + ridge first" order, cheapest first:
   - a per-persona lambda;
   - a target transform for the skew (ordinal or log-odds of fit >= 3);
   - k-nearest-neighbour fit over the embeddings, blended with the ridge;
   - more 3+ examples per narrow persona, by sampling gold shows near each persona's top-50 roots.
3. **Before any later `--predict`.** The RSS cache holds only the 3,009 gold feeds, so the other ~23k catalogued shows build `show-metadata` packs. Only about 6% of the rated gold shows are `show-metadata`. Run `enrich-rss.mjs --keys all` and `embed.mjs --keys all --embedder transformers` first, or expect a further transfer loss on those shows.

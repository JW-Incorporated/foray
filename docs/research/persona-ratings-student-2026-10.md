# Persona-rating student (RV-25, RV-32), 2026-10

Two cards of [`docs/roadmap/recommender-v1.md`](../roadmap/recommender-v1.md), issue #1380, wrote this report:

- **RV-25** trained RV-14's student (`tools/recommender/student.mjs`, ridge from features to the persona rating vector) on the RV-19 gold set minus its holdout ([`persona-ratings-gold-2026-10.md`](persona-ratings-gold-2026-10.md)). It then scored the student on the 495 rated holdout shows and failed the gate. That was the holdout's **first** look.
- **RV-32** is the orchestrator's follow-up of 2026-10-15. It made the gate ceiling-relative (an amendment under D4) and built student v2. The generated block below is RV-32's run. It is the holdout's **second** look.

The block between the `student:gate` markers is generated by `node tools/recommender/predict.mjs --train`. Everything outside it is written by hand. No model was called and nothing was fetched. The features are the committed catalogue files, the local RSS cache and the local RV-12 embeddings (`Xenova/bge-small-en-v1.5`, 384-dim, from the RV-19 run, copied and not re-embedded).

## How the holdout was used

- **This is the holdout's second look.** RV-25 scored ridge v1 on it once. RV-32 scored the one v2 configuration on it once.
- **No choice used it.** Every choice was made by 5-fold CV on the 2,483 training rows only:
  - the per-persona lambda;
  - the kNN's k and its blend weight;
  - whether to calibrate.

  The CV tables for every candidate are in the generated block. The code enforces this:
  - `chooseStudentConfig` reads only the rows it is given;
  - a test plants extreme holdout rows and asserts that every chosen hyperparameter and every CV score is unchanged.
- **The candidate grids were fixed before the run.** They are the orchestrator's: lambda on RV-25's 9-point grid, k in {5, 10, 20, 40}, blend weight in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75}, isotonic calibration or none.
  - A CV-only dry run on the training rows checked the runtime before the gate run. It never predicted a holdout row.
  - Nothing was changed after the holdout numbers were seen.

## Verdict: the gate still fails, so nothing is predicted

1. **Student v2 fails the ceiling-relative gate.** Two of the three deciding metrics miss:
   - Alpha on `fit` is 0.817 against 0.80. This **passes** now; it was 0.785 in RV-25.
   - Per-persona Spearman meets the persona's own bar for only 3 of 13 personas: banter-regular, practical-improver and mechanism-hunter.
   - Mean within-node tau is 0.524 against a bar of 0.562. It was 0.512 in RV-25.
2. **The two sub-scores that fail are the same as before.** `accessibility` (0.65) and `binge_vs_dip` (0.53) are dropped. `topic_fit` (0.82) and `depth` (0.68) are kept.
3. **`student-model.json` is committed with `gate.pass: false`.** `ratings-predicted.json` is not written, and `--predict` refuses. `--check` passes: the model is bound to the committed gold set and, because the kNN reads its neighbours' targets at predict time, to the committed `ratings.json` too.
4. **The ~23k other catalogued shows were not enriched or embedded.** Nothing would consume them while the gate fails. The orchestrator runs `enrich-rss --keys all` and `embed --keys all` when a gate passes.

## What student v2 changed

- **CV picked a non-trivial configuration for every persona.**
  - Lambda: 1,000 for all 13. The CV curve is the same shape as RV-25's, so a per-persona lambda bought nothing.
  - kNN: blended for all 13, with k 5 or 10 and weight 0.25 or 0.5.
  - Calibration: isotonic for all 13.
  - Together they cut the CV MSE on `fit` by 2-21% per persona against ridge alone (commute-explainer 2%, the rest 8-21%).
- **On the holdout, the gains are real but small.**
  - Spearman rose for 12 of 13 personas, by up to 0.04; mechanism-hunter fell 0.009.
  - MAE fell for every persona.
- **The calibration did what it was for.**
  - The mean of the student on the 3+ subset rose from 2.2-3.0 to 2.5-3.1.
  - The 3+ MAE fell from 0.5-1.1 to 0.5-1.0.
  - It is monotone, so it changes order only through the one-decimal rounding.
- **Ranking among good fits is still weak.**
  - On the 3+ subset, Spearman is 0.07-0.66.
  - Sharp-comedy is still 0.07: the student cannot order comedy shows for that persona.

## The ceilings: how they were computed, and a cross-check

- **The Spearman ceiling is committed data.** It is the gold report's mean-retest column (joint pass 1 vs 2 and 1 vs 3), parsed by `teacherRetestFrom` as in RV-25.
- **The within-node tau ceiling is derived, because no committed file keeps per-pass fits.**
  - `ratings.json` keeps only pass means and a pass count, and `rating-progress.json` keeps only reservation state.
  - So the ceiling comes from the same per-persona retest Spearman via the bivariate-normal identities (Kruskal 1958): r = 2 sin(pi rho / 6) and tau = (2 / pi) asin r, averaged over the 13 personas.
  - Each persona weighs the same because every holdout show is rated by every persona, so each contributes the same node groups. The result is 0.624, which gives a bar of min(0.60, 0.9 x 0.624) = 0.562.
  - The code is `gateCeilings` in `predict.mjs` and `ceilingsFromRetest` in `student.mjs`. `gate()` takes the ceilings as input.
- **Cross-check against uncommitted data: the derived tau ceiling is too low.**
  - The RV-19 results files are still in the RV-13 worktree's `data-local/` and are not committed. Measured on them, the teacher's own mean within-node tau between joint passes is 0.755 for passes 1 vs 2 (497 shows, 412 groups) and 0.751 for passes 1 vs 3 (421 groups).
  - On the measured ceiling the bar would be the 0.60 cap, not 0.562.
  - The derivation therefore makes the tau bar **easier** than the amendment intends.
  - It does not change this verdict: 0.524 fails both 0.562 and 0.60. It would matter if a later student landed between the two bars.

## What would have to change (not done here)

These are orchestrator follow-ups. None is in this card.

1. **The tau ceiling.**
   - Commit a measured within-node retest tau before any gate relies on the derived one. For example, `agreement.mjs` could add a joint-vs-joint within-node tau column to the gold report; that is the pilot's follow-up 3 widened.
   - Until then, treat a tau between 0.562 and 0.60 as a fail.
2. **The student.** The remaining candidates in D4 (d)'s "embedding + ridge first" order are:
   - a target transform for the skew: ordinal, or log-odds of fit >= 3;
   - more 3+ examples per narrow persona, by sampling gold shows near each persona's top-50 roots. History-deep-diver has 35 and true-crime-careful 14 in the holdout.
3. **The holdout has now been looked at twice.** A third gate run should either use a fresh holdout or say plainly that it is the third look.
